### The Next Wave Is Quiet Now... But Not For Long

![Pelle Larsson, an underrated rookie who performed well at the NBA Summer League](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:948/format:webp/0*zhP9gi7_vpIT4UzL)  
<span style="font-size:0.5em;">Pelle Larsson, an underrated rookie who performed well at the NBA Summer League. (NBA.com)</span>

Everyone has been watching *Bronny James*, *Bijan Robinson*, and *Cooper Flagg*, but the players who help you win your fantasy league? They are not on the viral highlight reels; they are the undervalued stars.

Every year, there is a *Jordan Addison* or *Jalen Williams* who slips under the radar, only to explode when the games matter. Hence, the next wave of undervalued rookies is taking shape quietly right now.

*NBA Summer League* and *NFL camps* are filled with late picks and overlooked rookies who are dominating reps, earning trust, and setting up breakout roles. If you blink, you’ll miss them, and someone else will stash them first.

### NBA Summer League Risers Worth Watching

![Pelle Larsson driving to the basket in Summer League](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/0*MKqw9ttzJjOLCD9a)  
<span style="font-size:0.5em;">Pelle Larsson driving to the basket in Summer League. (NBA.com)</span>

Starting with the NBA, the big names like *Flagg*, *Harper*, and *Bailey* will grab headlines. However, savvy fantasy managers are tracking guys like **Pelle Larsson**.

Larsson, the **44th pick out of Arizona**, has been one of the most efficient guards in Vegas. He’s shooting **over 50% from three**, playing high-IQ team ball, and logging serious minutes.

**Sallis**, the 2021 five-star who transferred to *Wake Forest*, is finally healthy and showing off elite midrange touch and defensive versatility. He just signed a **3-year $5.4 million contract** with the **Miami Heat**.

This type of contract is rare for second-round picks, who usually get two-way contracts or do not even make the NBA roster. Therefore, **Larsson is DFS gold waiting to happen**.

Even **Dillon Jones** (*Jazz*) and **Jalen Bridges** (*Nuggets*) are flashing switchable size and **fantasy-friendly stat profiles**. These are the players to flag now, especially in **deep dynasty** or **best ball**.

### NFL Rookies Flying Under the Radar

![Malik Washington, an upcoming Dolphins WR](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1050/format:webp/0*KFBY3SRADV0ZEJnZ)  
<span style="font-size:0.5em;">Malik Washington, an upcoming Dolphins WR. (Sun-Sentinel.com)</span>

On the NFL side, the noise is loud for top picks, but the value is buried in the late rounds. Keep an eye on **Malik Washington (WR, Dolphins)** and **Kimani Vidal (RB, Chargers)**.

Washington has been lighting up *Miami’s camp* with **clean routes** and **reliable hands**. With *Hill* and *Waddle* drawing attention, he could feast on soft zone coverage from the slot.

**Vidal**, a small-school RB out of *Troy*, is earning praise for his **pass-blocking** and **burst**. With *Ekeler* gone, there’s a real opportunity behind **Gus Edwards**.

Fantasy managers who grab these names now could be holding onto this year’s version of *Jayden Daniels* or *Puka Nacua*. They are **undrafted in fantasy**, but **league winners** in the end.

### One Man’s Trash is Another Man’s Treasure

![Kimani Vidal, a young Chargers RB](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1320/format:webp/0*RdqBQD8fAaXmTYur)

The edge in fantasy doesn’t come from copying top-10 lists. It comes from **seeing the next big thing before it becomes massive**.

That’s why paying attention to *Summer League*, *beat reporter tweets*, and *fringe camp reps* matters. That’s where breakouts are born.

Whether it’s *DFS steals* in October or *Week 4 waiver-wire wins*, the same truth holds: **fantasy leagues are won in the margins**. And the margins are filled with **quiet rookies putting in loud work**.

So ask yourself: *are you scouting stars, or are you scouting stats?*

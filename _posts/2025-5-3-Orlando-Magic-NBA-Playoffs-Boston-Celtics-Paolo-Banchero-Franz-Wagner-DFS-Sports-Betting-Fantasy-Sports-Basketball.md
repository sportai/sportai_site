Franz Wagner and Paolo Banchero were not supposed to be fantasy playoff stars. They were supposed to be learning on the fly, outmatched by a seasoned Celtics squad.

Instead, they exploded in Game 3, combining for **61 points** to beat the defending champs 95–93. DFS players who rolled with them at under $6000 apiece struck gold.

Boston’s defense was no joke, but the Magic outmuscled them and made every possession count. The duo scored efficiently and racked up fantasy points through rebounds and assists.

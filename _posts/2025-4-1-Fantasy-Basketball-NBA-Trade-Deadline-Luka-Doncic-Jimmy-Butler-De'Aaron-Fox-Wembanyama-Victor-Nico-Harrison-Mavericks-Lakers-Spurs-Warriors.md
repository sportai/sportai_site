<span style="font-size:3em;">O</span>ver a month after the 2025 NBA trade deadline has passed, it is safe to say that it was one of the **best in history**. **Superstars were traded, tanking franchises got worse**, and overall the **league was shaken up**. 

#### Luka Doncic Trade

**A trade on the magnitude of the Luka Doncic deal may never happen again.** On February 2nd, 2025, the blockbuster 3-team trade happened between the Los Angeles Lakers, Dallas Mavericks, and Utah Jazz. 

The trade sent **Luka Doncic to the Lakers, Anthony Davis, and Max Christie to the Mavericks**, and the Jazz received second-round picks. Since the trade occurred, the Lakers have jumped to becoming a **top seed** in the Western Conference, while the Mavericks will likely miss the playoffs. 

Intriguingly, Mavericks GM Nico Harrison did not explore trading Luka with any other teams than the Lakers. As a result, the Mavericks have a **dim future** since star guard Kyrie Irving tore his ACL and Anthony Davis got injured. 

Overall, the Lakers **won this trade and now have their next star** until Luka retires, while the Mavericks will **regret this trade for the rest of Luka’s career**. 

#### Jimmy Butler Deal to the Warriors

![A photograph of Jimmy Butler, Warriors Guard, smiling happily during a game.](https://a.espncdn.com/photo/2025/0313/r1463896_1296x729_16-9.jpg)
<span style="font-size:0.5em;">A photograph of Jimmy Butler, Warriors Forward, smiling happily during a game. (Athlete: Jimmy Butler Source: abc7news.com Photograph by abc7news, Date: March 15th, 2025)</span> 

At first, many **doubted the effectiveness** of bringing 35-year-old Jimmy Butler to the aging Golden State Warriors. Many critics were concerned with Butler’s ability to play at a high level and contribute to the same level as his **newly signed two-year, $101 million contract**. 

Overall, the five-team deal consisted mainly of the Miami Heat and Golden State Warriors, who swapped **Andrew Wiggins for Butler**, along with trading away some quality role players like Kyle Anderson. Since the trade, the Warriors have been electric with Butler, **going 18-5 and elevating themselves to the sixth seed**. 

Interestingly, the Warriors have gone **17-2 when Curry and Butler play together**, suggesting that they could make a run for the NBA Finals. Ultimately, **Butler’s ability to draw free throws gives Curry a natural break in the game.**. 

Butler also commands the bench and can take over games to achieve victory. Still, the most important piece of this trade has not happened yet, and that is **playoff Jimmy Butler.**

#### De'aaron Fox Deal to the Spurs

![A photograph of De’Aaron Fox, Spurs Guard, smiling happily during a game. ](https://library.sportingnews.com/styles/twitter_card_120x120/s3/2025-02/De%27Aaron%20Fox%20%28AP%29.jpg?itok=y5D8aLbn)
<span style="font-size:0.5em;">A photograph of De’Aaron Fox, Spurs Guard, smiling happily during a game. (Athlete: De’Aaron Fox Source: sportingnews.com Photograph by sportingnews, Date: March 7th, 2025)</span> 

Another massive trade during the 2025 NBA trade deadline was the Kings sending first-round picks and role players for De’aaron Fox, **a shifty and outstanding guard to pair with generational superstar Victor Wembanyama**. This deal was a three-team trade between the Spurs, Kings, and Bulls. 

The Kings received Zach LaVine and multiple first-round picks while the Bulls got their 2025 first-round pick back, along with solid role players. The Kings **have seen little improvement since the trade, barely maintaining a play-in spot at the tenth seed in the Western Conference**. 

The Bulls are also the tenth seed in the Eastern Conference despite having a subpar record. Unfortunately, the Spurs decided to give up on the season after there was news that their star, Victor Webanyama, **would miss the rest of 2025 due to developing blood clots**. 

Ultimately, Webanyama should be able to recover quickly due to his young age and his excellent focus on taking care of his body, illuminating the **immense promise the Spurs duo has in the future**.
